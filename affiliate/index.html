Affiliate terms · current version

The important bits.

Full programme terms are accepted in your affiliate dashboard.

Lifetime attribution

One valid referrer is attached to a player. That attribution does not expire.

Commission basis

Commission is calculated from eligible TH3FLOW platform fees actually charged to the referred player — not deposits, stakes, losses or turnover.

Qualification

A referral becomes active after a valid settled race. Fraud, multi-accounting and manipulated activity can be excluded.

Claim rules

Self-referrals are blocked. A referral must be claimed within the configured claim window and before the player's first race.

Promotion

No misleading win or earnings claims, spam, under-age targeting, undisclosed affiliate promotion or presenting TH3FLOW as an investment product.

Payouts

Only accrued affiliate balances are payable. Payout requests may be reviewed before completion.

Affiliate balances are separate from gameplay funds. Public tier rates can change for future fees; any bespoke creator rate is recorded to the affiliate account.
