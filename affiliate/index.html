Programme terms · version 2026-09-11-lifetime

What you are agreeing to.

The full commercial relationship is recorded when you join inside the dashboard. These are the launch principles the product enforces.

Attribution

One referrer per player. Self-referrals are blocked. The code must be claimed inside the configured claim window and before that player enters their first race.

Commission basis

Commission is a percentage of the eligible TH3FLOW platform fee actually charged to the referred player — not their stake, deposit, losses or total turnover.

Window

A valid referral attribution is lifetime. As long as the referred player remains validly attributed to you, eligible TH3FLOW platform fees they generate can continue to earn commission with no time expiry.

Qualification

Public tier progression counts referred players who complete a valid settled race. TH3FLOW may exclude fraud, multi-accounting or manipulated activity.

Promotion

No misleading earnings or win claims, impersonation, spam, under-age targeting, undisclosed affiliate promotion, or advertising that misrepresents TH3FLOW as an investment product.

Payouts

Only accrued affiliate balances are payable. Launch payouts may be manually reviewed while automated payout rails are being connected.

This page is the product implementation of the programme, not a substitute for final legal wording. Before public launch, TH3FLOW should have the complete affiliate terms reviewed alongside the wider gaming terms by local counsel.
TH3FLOW affiliate earnings are marketing commissions. Gameplay achievements and season progression remain non-cash. Rates are configurable and any bespoke creator rate is recorded separately.
